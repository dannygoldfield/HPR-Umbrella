# Creative principles

## Automate everything except taste

The system should remove repetitive labor without automating the final artistic decision.

## Generate possibilities, not answers

Variation is useful because it presents combinations the artist may not have chosen in advance. A generated candidate is an option for consideration, not a recommendation.

## Let the portrait do the central work

Movement and sound should make a portrait feel present without explaining the person or forcing a narrative.

## Preserve the loop

The transition from the last frame to the first is part of the work. Motion, texture, and sound are designed to return naturally to their beginnings.

## Preserve provenance

A candidate should never become mysterious merely because it was generated. Recipes, versions, ingredients, presets, and seeds remain inspectable.

## Keep private media private

Portraits, licensed sound libraries, generated candidates, workbooks, and production analytics remain outside the public source repository.
